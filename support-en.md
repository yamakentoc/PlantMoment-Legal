## Common questions

### I cannot access my Pro purchase

Check that you are using the same Apple Account used for the purchase, then try **Restore purchases** in the app. If that does not help, contact us with the plan you purchased and a description of the problem.

### How do I manage my monthly subscription?

On your iPhone, go to **Settings → your name → Subscriptions** to check or cancel a subscription. Apple's procedures apply to billing and refunds.

### How do I move my data to a new device or restore it after reinstalling?

PlantMoment does not automatically sync data between devices. If you have a backup file that you created and kept on your previous device, use **Settings → Backup and restore** in the app on your new device. Restoring replaces the app data currently on that device, so review the confirmation screen first.
